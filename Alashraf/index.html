Who We Are

Working Together for a Better Community

Al-Ashraf Organization for Development is a Sudanese voluntary, non-profit organization working in humanitarian action, community development and volunteer service.

The organization was established in 2005 and is registered with the Humanitarian Aid Commission (HAC).

  • Humanitarian and community support
  • Volunteer and youth development
  • Health and community initiatives
  • Environmental sanitation activities

What We Do

Our Fields of Work

Our work focuses on key areas that contribute to community resilience, humanitarian response and sustainable development.

Food Security

Supporting vulnerable families and contributing to access to essential food supplies.

Education & Training

Training youth and volunteers in humanitarian, health and community service skills.

Community Development

Supporting communities to build resilience and create positive local change.

Health Care

Supporting patients, health facilities and people in need of essential care.

WASH

Environmental sanitation, public cleanliness and tree-planting campaigns.
